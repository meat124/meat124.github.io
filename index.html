Hyunjin Park

I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University, advised by Prof. Jongeun Choi. I also obtained a B.S. degree in Mechanical Engineering from Yonsei University.

My research interests lie in Robotic Foundation Models. I aim to build generalist robot policies on top of foundation models — robots that can work alongside people and help out in our everyday lives.
